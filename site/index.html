Your records, explained

There is no account or automatic sync. Before changing phones or deleting the app, export a backup from Settings and keep it somewhere safe. Restore that file on your new device.

Rest timer alerts require notification permission. Live Activities require iOS 16.2 or later, and Dynamic Island requires a supported iPhone.
